Autonomous content operations, structured like a company

Built on PaperClip — the open-source agent orchestrator that organizes AI agents into a working company with roles, budgets, and governance. Launched in early 2026, it crossed 50,000+ GitHub stars within weeks and has become the reference architecture for multi-agent systems.

My implementation runs a complete content pipeline for my own brand: a research agent tracks AI and automation trends, a strategist agent turns them into content angles, a writer agent drafts, a review agent QA-checks tone and accuracy, and a publishing agent prepares posts for scheduling — with per-agent token budgets so costs never run away.

It's a live testbed for the exact question my consulting answers: how far can you safely push autonomy in a real business process? Every lesson from running it flows into client recommendations.

About

From CAD drawings to AI agent teams

I began as a mechanical CAD engineer and instructor — designing products and teaching hundreds of students to think in systems. The habit of breaking complex things into precise, repeatable steps never left.

It carried me through growth marketing, then into B2B SaaS, where I progressed from Technical Analyst to QA Engineer to Solutions Engineer — owning technical solutions end to end: pre-sales discovery, solution design, build, launch, and support for enterprise teams of 50–100+ users.

Automation only saves time if it doesn't break. So I build systems that don't.

The QA chapter shaped how I build today: edge-case thinking, retry logic, and error instrumentation by default. Now I share what I've learned and help founders, marketers, and operations teams find the hours hiding in their workflows.
